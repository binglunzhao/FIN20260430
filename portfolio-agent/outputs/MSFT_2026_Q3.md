## Earnings Brief — MSFT Q3 FY2026 (Apr 29, 2026)

### 1. One-line verdict
Beat across the board — revenue, operating income, and EPS all exceeded expectations, with Azure growth of 40% (constant currency) ahead of guidance and Microsoft 365 Copilot paid seats surging to 20 million, up 250% YoY.

### 2. Key numbers
- Revenue: $82.9B, +18% YoY (+15% CC) — beat; all three segments ahead of expectations
- Microsoft Cloud revenue: $54.5B, +29% YoY; AI business ARR $37B, +123% YoY
- Gross margin: 68%, down YoY (AI infrastructure investment); Cloud GM 66% (slightly better than expected)
- EPS: $4.27, +21% YoY (+18% CC, adjusted for OpenAI) — beat expectations
- Free cash flow: $15.8B (compressed by $31.9B CapEx quarter)
- Q4 guidance: revenue $86.7–$87.8B (13–15% growth); Azure 39–40% CC; FY27 double-digit revenue + OI growth

### 3. Management tone
Overall confidence: HIGH — no hedging on AI demand narrative; structural platform shift framing.
Key phrases:
"We are at the beginning of one of the most consequential platform shifts that will change the entire tech stack as agents proliferate."
"Weekly [Copilot] engagement is now at the same level as Outlook."

### 4. What analysts asked about
1. Demand sustainability / who pays for AI (Weiss/Morgan Stanley): Satya explained per-seat → per-seat + usage shift; Hood noted bookings will look more like Azure meter over time.
2. CapEx magnitude and ROI confidence (Keirstead/UBS, Moerdler/Bernstein): Hood defended with $600B+ RPO backlog and accelerating demand; transparent on $25B component pricing headwind.
3. AI margins vs. costs (Thill/Jefferies): Usage-based pricing, first-party silicon, and OpenAI IP effectively free on margin driving better-than-feared margins.

### 5. Red flags (if any)
- Gaming: Xbox content & services down 5–7% CC; Q4 guided decline low-teens; impairment charges hit opex.
- Windows OEM Q4: high-teens decline (W10 comparable, inventory normalization, memory-driven PC prices).
- Capacity constrained "at least through 2026" despite $190B CY2026 CapEx plan — execution risk at scale.
- OpenAI agreement restructured: Satya diplomatic but short on specifics; watch for Q4 disclosure.

### 6. Watch list for next quarter
1. Azure Q4 actual vs. 39–40% guide — early capacity delivery was the Q3 upside driver; repeat execution is the key bull signal.
2. M365 Copilot net seat adds — Hood guided sequential increase; acceleration into consumption model is the key SaaS metric.
3. Free cash flow — $15.8B depressed by CapEx ramp; watch Q4 $40B+ CapEx impact vs. RPO collections.
