## Earnings Brief — AAPL Q2 FY2026 (Apr 30, 2026)

### 1. One-line verdict
Strong beat — $111.2B revenue (+17% YoY) set a new March record despite meaningful iPhone and Mac supply constraints, with iPhone +22% and EPS +22%; the biggest surprise was MacBook Neo demand described as "off the charts," straining supply for several months ahead.

### 2. Key numbers
- Revenue: $111.2B, +17% YoY — March record; FX +2.5pt tailwind; beat on supply-constrained basis
- iPhone: $57B, +22% YoY; March record for upgraders; 99% satisfaction (451 Research)
- Services: $31B, +16% YoY; double-digit growth in vast majority of markets
- Mac: $8.4B, +6% YoY — constrained by SoC availability and Neo demand surge
- Gross margin: 49.3%, above high end of guidance; +110 bps sequential — beat
- EPS: $2.10, +22% YoY — March record; beat expectations
- Operating cash flow: $28.7B
- Q3 color: Mac supply constrained several months; memory costs "significantly higher" in June and escalating; $100B incremental buyback; net-cash-neutral target abandoned

### 3. Management tone
Overall confidence: HIGH on iPhone/Services; CAUTIOUS on memory costs beyond June. No prior brief for comparison.
Key phrases:
"The customer response to MacBook Neo has just been off the charts, with higher-than-expected demand."
"Beyond the June quarter, we believe memory costs will drive an increasing impact on our business."
Tim Cook deflected twice on memory pricing strategy — deliberate signal that options are still open.

### 4. What analysts asked about
1. Supply constraints magnitude (Woodring/Morgan Stanley): Mac mini, Studio, Neo take "several months" to balance; iPhone clear for June — most operational detail on the call.
2. Memory cost trajectory (Reitzes/Melius, Mohan/BofA): Tim confirmed escalating impact but refused to signal pricing response. Moderate deflection.
3. AI model strategy (Mohan, Chatterjee/JPMorgan): Google collaboration "going well"; R&D growing faster than company. No product specifics given.

### 5. Red flags (if any)
- Memory cost escalation: "significantly higher" in Q3, "increasing" beyond — Apple may absorb rather than raise prices; watch gross margin.
- Mac supply crunch lasting months: delayed revenue on record Neo demand.
- CEO transition: Tim Cook flagged this is one of his last earnings calls; John succession confirmed.
- Net cash neutral target dropped after 8 years — capital allocation framework unclear.

### 6. Watch list for next quarter
1. June gross margin — memory cost absorption vs. carry-in inventory offset is the key variable.
2. Mac Q3 revenue — Neo/Mac mini/Studio supply unlock speed determines Services attach rate from new customers.
3. Services advertising growth — App Store new inventory + Maps ads in US/Canada launch this summer.
